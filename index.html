Standard 5-Step Deployment Lifecycle

  1. 01. RF & Physical Site Survey: Spectrum heatmap, heat dissipation analysis and cable pathway routing.
  2. 02. Topology Design: Spine-leaf or core-distribution-access architecture with VRRP/HSRP redundancy.
  3. 03. Hardware Staging: Out-of-band management configuration, firmware hardening, and port isolation.
  4. 04. Physical Installation: Neat-patch rack mounting, structured cable comb dressing, labeled patch panels.
  5. 05. Certification & Handover: 100% link test reports, disaster failover dry-run, and NOC integration.
